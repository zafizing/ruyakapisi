Why this costs something

Because the amount is not a fee. It is the second half of your answer. The first half says what you are waiting for. The second says what that waiting weighs. Both are shown on your entry, both are permanent, and you can sort the whole line by either one.

A sentence that costs nothing is a sentence you post and forget. This one you decide on. That decision — five dollars or five hundred — is the part that means something, and it is the reason anybody reads this line at all.

Paying more moves nobody forward. Nothing moves anybody forward. It changes how heavily your answer sits in the line, and nothing else.
