      Who we are

      We are the people in the line.

      Everyone is waiting for something. A phone call, a visa, a test result, an apology, the end of winter. Most of it we carry alone, and most days we never say it out loud.

      The Queue is a line with nothing at the end of it. You write one sentence, in any language, about what you are waiting for and you take a place. There is no prize, no draw, no door that opens. If a sentence is not in English, you can translate it with one tap.

      There is also one person behind it, [your name], who keeps the line honest. Nothing is ever moved forward, and nobody can pay to get ahead of anyone.

      Joining is free. If you would like to leave something to keep the line going, you can. It turns your footprints brass and changes nothing else.

      Privacy and removals. We store what you type here. Please do not write other people's names or personal details. You can delete your own entry from the device you wrote it on (open it and press Delete), or ask us to remove your name and we will; the sentence stays, anonymous.
